I am a PhD candidate in Electrical & Computing Engineering at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/),
under the co-supervision of Professor [Derek Nowrouzezahrai](https://cim.mcgill.ca/~derek/)
and Professor [Paul G. Kry](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~kry/).

I am also currently working as an intern researcher at [Ubisoft La Forge](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/studio/laforge), mentored by Dr. [Damien Rioux-Lavoie](https://arnaudschoentgen.github.io) and Dr. [Arnaud Schoentgen](https://arnaudschoentgen.github.io).

My research interest spans physics-based animation, such as fluid simulations and deformables, and intersections between ML techniques and computer graphics methods.
